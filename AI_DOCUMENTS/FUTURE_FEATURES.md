# SFM PRO Enterprise

# Future Features

**Document Version:** 1.0

**Application Version:** v6.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision and future enhancements planned for SFM PRO Enterprise.

It serves as the strategic roadmap for expanding the application beyond the current version while maintaining scalability, usability, and enterprise-quality standards.

---

# Product Vision

SFM PRO Enterprise aims to become an intelligent financial management platform that helps individuals and families manage their complete financial ecosystem.

The application should evolve from a LocalStorage-based solution into a secure, cloud-enabled, AI-powered finance platform.
