# SFM PRO Enterprise

## User Guide — v3.5 Stable

Welcome to SFM PRO Enterprise, a local personal-finance workspace for recording financial activity, monitoring commitments, and reviewing progress in one place. This guide explains how to use each area of the application and how to keep your records protected.

> **Important:** SFM PRO stores financial records in the current browser profile. It does not automatically sync records between browsers or devices. Export a backup before clearing browser data, changing devices, or performing browser maintenance.

## Introduction

SFM PRO Enterprise brings day-to-day financial planning into a single workspace. Use it to record income and expenses, set budgets, follow loans and credit cards, review investments, and keep due dates visible.

### What you can manage

| Area | What it helps you do |
| --- | --- |
| Income | Record earnings and review income history. |
| Expense | Capture spending by category, payment method, and date. |
| Budget | Set monthly category limits and track utilization. |
| Loans | Monitor loan balances, terms, and repayment commitments. |
| Credit Cards | Track card limits, outstanding balances, due dates, and card activity. |
| EMI | Maintain installment schedules and payment status. |
| Investments | Record invested value, current value, and performance. |
| Reports | Review summary metrics and visual financial analysis. |
| Goals | Track personal financial targets and progress. |
| Calendar and recurring transactions | Plan dates, reminders, and repeated financial activity. |
| Backup & Restore | Export and safely restore your local data. |

### Getting started

1. Sign in to open the dashboard.
2. Add your income and expense records first.
3. Create budgets for the categories you want to control.
4. Add loans, credit cards, EMIs, investments, and goals as relevant.
5. Review the dashboard regularly and export backups at sensible intervals.

## Login

Open the application’s login page and enter the username and password provided for your SFM PRO workspace. Select **Login** to continue.

If the page does not accept your credentials:

- Check spelling, capitalization, and accidental spaces.
- Refresh the page and enter the values again.
- Contact the person who configured the local application if the issue continues.

For shared computers, do not save credentials in the browser, and always log out when you are finished.

## Dashboard

The dashboard is the central workspace. The navigation sidebar opens the dedicated finance modules, while the dashboard itself brings high-value information together.

### Welcome, search, and navigation

Use the sidebar to open Income, Expense, Budget, Loans, Credit Cards, EMI Tracker, Investments, Reports, and Settings. The dashboard search field helps you quickly find matching transaction, loan, or budget information where supported.

The header includes workspace controls such as the navigation toggle and theme control. On smaller screens, use the menu button to open or close the sidebar.

### Balance and KPI cards

The balance cards provide a quick view of financial totals. KPI cards summarize important indicators such as total income, total expense, outstanding loans, budget remaining, upcoming EMI, or other relevant metrics. Treat these cards as a summary; open the related module to review and maintain the underlying records.

### Monthly summary and analytics

The Monthly Summary compares current-period income, expenses, savings, and budget use. Financial Analytics and Financial Trends use charts and summary values to make patterns easier to spot:

- Compare income with expense activity.
- Review spending by category.
- Watch budget utilization.
- Monitor loan-to-income and related financial-health indicators.

Charts show the information currently available in your records. If a chart has no data, add records in the corresponding module and refresh the dashboard view.

### Smart Insights and financial health

Smart Insights highlight relevant patterns, such as changes in spending or approaching commitments. Financial Health and Budget Progress present broader indicators that can help you assess financial discipline over time. Use these as planning aids, not as financial or investment advice.

### Quick Actions and recent transactions

Quick Actions provide direct routes to common tasks such as adding income, adding expenses, reviewing budgets, loans, investments, or reports. Recent Transactions lists the latest recorded activity. Use search and filtering controls, where available, to narrow the list.

### Dashboard notifications

The Notification Center displays financial alerts within the application interface. Notifications may include reminders, due-date context, or record-related updates. Notification settings control the local display preference only; SFM PRO does not send email, push, or background notifications.

## Managing financial records

Each finance module uses a form for entering records and a table or summary area for reviewing them. Complete required fields before saving. Enter meaningful names, valid dates, and positive amounts where applicable so summaries and reports remain useful.

### Income

Open **Income** from the dashboard sidebar to record earnings.

1. Enter an income title or source.
2. Choose a category.
3. Enter the amount and date.
4. Add notes when they provide useful context.
5. Select **Save Income**.

The Income Summary and Income History help you review recorded income. Use search or sort controls to locate older entries. Correct inaccurate entries from the module’s available record controls rather than creating duplicate replacements.

### Expense

Open **Expenses** to record spending.

1. Enter an expense title.
2. Choose the applicable category and payment method.
3. Enter a valid amount and transaction date.
4. Add notes if needed.
5. Select **Save Expense**.

The expense summary shows spending totals and the expense table displays saved records. Use the category, payment, search, and sort controls to focus on the information you need.

### Budget

Open **Budget** to set monthly spending limits by category.

1. Choose a budget category.
2. Enter the monthly budget amount.
3. Provide the requested date or period information.
4. Select **Save Budget**.

The module displays total budget, budget used, remaining amount, and utilization. Review budget progress regularly after entering expenses. A budget is most useful when the category naming is consistent with your expense records.

### Loans

Open **Loans** to maintain loan commitments.

1. Enter the loan name and lender or bank.
2. Enter principal amount, interest rate, tenure, and start date as requested.
3. Save the loan record.

Loan summaries present totals and EMI-related information. Keep loan terms accurate so dashboard and report summaries have a reliable basis. Update the original record if the terms change, and avoid creating duplicate copies of the same loan.

### Credit Cards

Open **Credit Cards** to track card accounts.

1. Add the card name or issuer details.
2. Enter the credit limit, outstanding balance, due date, and any other requested details.
3. Save the card.

Use the card table to review cards, balances, and due dates. Maintain current balances and payment information to make alerts and financial-health summaries meaningful.

### EMI

Open **EMI Tracker** to manage installment commitments.

1. Enter an EMI name.
2. Enter the monthly EMI amount and requested schedule details.
3. Save the record.

The EMI summary separates monthly, paid, and pending amounts. The schedule table can be searched to find an installment quickly. Keep payment status current after each payment so due-date visibility remains accurate.

### Investments

Open **Investments** to record holdings.

1. Enter the investment name and type.
2. Enter the broker or platform, invested amount, current value, and investment date.
3. Save the investment.

The investment overview displays total invested value and available record details. Update current values as needed to keep performance and report views representative. SFM PRO records the values you enter; it does not retrieve market prices automatically.

### Reports

Open **Reports** to review consolidated financial information.

Use report filters to select the period, category, or module information you want to analyze. The report summary may include income, expenses, balances, investments, and charts such as income-versus-expense trends.

Reports reflect the records stored in this browser. If totals are unexpected, first check the relevant finance module for missing, duplicate, or incorrectly dated entries.

## Planning and tracking

### Financial Goals

The **Financial Goals** dashboard section helps you define and track targets such as savings, purchases, or debt-reduction objectives.

1. Add a clear goal name.
2. Enter a target amount and any requested target date.
3. Save the goal.
4. Update progress as you contribute toward it.

Use the goal cards to compare current progress with the target. Give goals distinct names so each remains easy to identify.

### Notifications

The Notification Center presents alerts inside the application. Review it regularly for reminders and financial context. In Settings, you can choose whether the Notification Center and due-date reminders appear in the application interface.

These preferences are display controls only. They do not send communications outside the application.

### Financial Calendar

The **Financial Calendar** section brings important financial dates into one place. Use it to review recorded events, upcoming activity, and date-based reminders. Add or update relevant calendar information through the available calendar controls, then check the dashboard summary for upcoming items.

Use dates consistently and verify the selected year, month, and day before saving. A valid date is especially important for reports, recurring activity, and reminders.

### Recurring Transactions

Recurring Transactions reduce repeated manual entry for predictable activity.

1. Choose the recurring type, such as Income, Expense, or EMI Reminder.
2. Enter the name, amount, category, frequency, and start date requested by the form.
3. Save the template.

You can search the recurring list and use the provided edit or cancel controls when changes are needed. Review templates periodically, particularly after an amount, account, or schedule changes, to avoid outdated recurring entries.

## Backup & Restore

Backup & Restore protects the records stored in your browser. It is available from the dashboard and through the shortcut in Settings.

### Export a backup

1. Open the **Backup & Restore** section on the dashboard.
2. Select **Export Backup**.
3. Save the generated JSON file in a secure location that is not limited to the same browser profile.
4. Use a clear file name that includes the date, such as <code>sfm-pro-backup-2026-07-20.json</code>.

An export captures supported financial data and related application records. Keep backups private because the file may contain sensitive financial information.

### Restore a backup

1. Export a new backup of the current browser data first, especially if it is important.
2. Select **Import Backup**.
3. Choose a valid SFM PRO backup JSON file.
4. Review the import preview or summary.
5. Select **Confirm Restore** only when you are certain the backup is the intended one.

The application validates the file before restoration and displays a friendly message when it cannot be used. Do not rename an unrelated text file to JSON or edit a backup by hand unless you understand the required structure.

### Safe backup practice

- Export before clearing site data, browser cache, or local storage.
- Keep more than one dated backup in a secure location.
- Test an important backup on a separate browser profile only when you can do so safely.
- Do not send backup files through unsecured channels.
- Restore only files you trust and that were created by a compatible SFM PRO version.

Supported backup formats include the compatible v3.3 through v3.5 range. An unsupported backup version displays a compatibility warning instead of being restored.

## Settings

Open **Settings** from the dashboard sidebar to personalize your local workspace. Settings change presentation preferences and do not delete or alter financial records.

### Application Preferences

Choose the preferred:

| Preference | Available options |
| --- | --- |
| Currency | INR, USD, EUR, or GBP |
| Date format | DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY, or YYYY-MM-DD |
| Decimal places | 0, 2, or 3 |

Select **Save Preferences** after choosing valid options. The page confirms whether the preferences were saved for the current browser.

### Theme

Choose **Light** or **Dark**. The selection previews on the page and is saved when you select **Save Preferences**. Use a theme with adequate contrast for your environment.

### Notifications

Use the toggles to choose whether the Notification Center and due-date reminders are displayed in the application UI. These are local display preferences only; they do not enable email, push, or background notifications.

### Data Management

The Backup & Restore shortcut returns you to the dashboard’s secure data workflow. Settings intentionally does not contain a direct delete-all-data action, helping prevent accidental loss of financial records.

### Restore Defaults

Select **Restore Defaults** to return settings to their standard values:

- Currency: INR
- Date format: DD/MM/YYYY
- Decimal places: 2
- Theme: Light
- Notification Center: enabled
- Due-date reminders: enabled

This affects settings only, not your financial records.

### About SFM PRO

The About area identifies the product as **SFM PRO Enterprise**, release **v3.5 Stable**, and shows backup compatibility information. It is informational and does not require saving.

## Logout

Use the **Logout** control in the dashboard header when you have finished your session. Logout returns you to the login page and prevents another person using the same browser window from continuing in your session.

On a shared device:

1. Finish any record you are editing.
2. Export a backup if you made important changes.
3. Select **Logout**.
4. Close the browser window if it is not your device.

Logging out does not erase your locally stored financial records. Browser data-management actions can erase them, which is why backups are essential.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is my data stored?

Financial records and preferences are stored in the current browser’s local storage. They remain on that browser profile unless you remove browser data, restore another backup, or use a supported data-management workflow.

### Can I use the same data on another computer?

Yes, by exporting a backup from the original browser and restoring it in the destination browser. Create a fresh backup before moving data and store the file securely during transfer.

### Why did my dashboard total not change as expected?

Check that the record was saved, that its date belongs to the expected period, and that the amount and category are correct. Then review the relevant module table for duplicates or missing records.

### Why is a chart empty?

A chart needs relevant valid data. Add records in the related module, ensure dates are valid, and return to the dashboard or reports page to review the updated view.

### Can Settings delete my financial records?

No. Settings changes local preferences only. Use Backup & Restore to protect records before any browser-data maintenance.

### Why are notifications not appearing outside the app?

SFM PRO notification settings control in-application display only. The application does not send email, operating-system push, or background notifications.

### Why was my backup rejected?

The file may be empty, malformed, incompatible, too large, or not a valid SFM PRO backup. Select a trusted JSON file produced by a compatible version of SFM PRO.

### Can I edit the backup JSON file manually?

It is not recommended. Manual edits can make a backup invalid or remove required fields. Preserve the exported file as generated and keep dated copies.

### What happens if I clear browser data?

Local records can be permanently removed. Export a backup before clearing site data, cookies, cache, or local storage. Restore the backup afterward if needed.

### Does SFM PRO provide financial advice?

No. Summaries, charts, budgets, and health indicators are organizational tools. Make financial, lending, investment, and tax decisions with appropriate independent advice.

## Tips

1. **Record activity promptly.** Add income, expenses, and payments close to the transaction date to keep reports dependable.
2. **Use consistent names and categories.** Consistency makes search, filters, budgets, and reports more useful.
3. **Review due dates weekly.** Check EMI, loan, credit-card, calendar, and recurring-transaction information before payments become urgent.
4. **Set realistic budgets.** Start with recent spending patterns, then refine limits over time.
5. **Keep investment values current.** Record updates when you want reports to reflect the latest known values.
6. **Review the dashboard regularly.** Trends and utilization are easier to act on when viewed consistently.
7. **Back up before major changes.** Export before restoring another file, changing devices, or clearing browser data.
8. **Use a secure backup location.** Financial backups should be protected with your normal device and file-security practices.
9. **Use keyboard navigation when helpful.** Standard controls can be reached with Tab, activated with Enter or Space, and read by compatible assistive technology.
10. **Log out on shared devices.** This is a simple final step that protects your workspace from casual access.

## Support checklist

If you encounter a problem, capture the following before seeking support:

- The module and action you were using.
- The exact message shown by the application.
- Whether the issue occurs after refreshing the page.
- Whether you recently restored a backup or cleared browser data.
- A current exported backup, stored securely, if data review is required.

Do not share passwords or unprotected backup files when requesting assistance.
